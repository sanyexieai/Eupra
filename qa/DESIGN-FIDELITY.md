## Highest-impact fidelity actions

1. **Restore WEB01's missing sections and alternating light/dark rhythm.** The original has a light how-to band containing three illustrated white cards, a separate story-relay section with paired artwork, a playable-video section, and a large framed closing CTA. The runtime uses dark text-only how-to columns, omits relay/video sections and uses an unframed closing slogan. This is a structural gap on both desktop and mobile, not spacing polish. Use supplied assets as labelled examples, and retain truthful generation/video limitations.
2. **Top-align CN09's desktop artwork.** Its image card is vertically centered against the long right-side provenance column, starting roughly 450 CSS pixels below the right-hand title and leaving the upper-left region empty. Align primary content at the top and move/disclose extended graph, contribution roles and technical IDs below the main detail block. Do not remove genuine provenance or privacy controls.
3. **Restore contextual page chrome and avoid duplicate narratives.** The shared dark website header, full-width demo banner and footer dominate pale CN02/03/06/11 and add height across task screens. Contextual light/dark navigation can preserve service honesty with a compact status label. CN04/09/10 repeat focal titles; CN07 repeats its body paragraph. These changes are reported locally but remain unverified in pixels.
4. **Preserve asset framing, not just asset identity.** CN01's desktop portal is cropped at the upper frame and largely loses the foreground silhouette behind its CTA. CN11's girl/CEO/moon layering and crops differ from the supplied collage. Match source focal positions while retaining an explicit decorative-example label; do not fill private history with sample artwork.
5. **Restore selected typography and visual hierarchy.** CN02's original headline emphasizes “发生” in purple; the runtime makes the whole heading black. CN03's desktop theatre is a shallow landscape box, with a small central mark, rather than the original near-square focal stage. CN07 lacks the contrasting white participation card. CN13's two-credit visual becomes one continuous bar rather than the original two segments. These differences can be corrected without pretending unavailable services are ready.

## Cross-page asset and typography checks

- Asset provenance alone is insufficient: match aspect ratio, object position, silhouette visibility, overlap order, corner radii, border weight and background gradients in the next screenshot comparison.
- The baseline uses the newer luminous sphere in global chrome and several stages, but legacy square-ring marks remain in CN04/CN09 placeholders and some version/credit tiles. Decide which approved identity belongs to which role and apply it consistently; do not silently assume the old mockup mark must replace a user-approved new logo.
- Runtime Chinese headings are generally bold system sans while some reference titles use a lighter/less condensed appearance. Establish page-specific type scale, weight and line-height from intact references; do not copy missing glyphs. Important tiny status/helper captions need readability as well as visual similarity.
- Preserve absent-media states separately from official examples. A labelled official-example route can demonstrate original crops and artwork; the private-result route must not borrow it as supposedly generated output.
